## Problem

Instagram wants to increase **average time spent per user per day** by scaling Reels. However, sustainable Reels growth depends on a healthy and consistent **creator supply**. The challenge is to define the right creator-side metrics to determine whether Reels is building a sustainable content ecosystem.

## Objective

Identify and prioritize **creator-focused metrics** across the complete Reels creator journey — from discovery and first creation to publishing, repeat creation, retention, monetization, and content quality.

## Framework

**Creator Metrics Framework** — a **North Star Metric (NSM) + OMTM + funnel metrics + guardrails** framework covering:

- Awareness & Acquisition
- Activation
- Engagement
- Retention
- Revenue
- Referral
- Quality & Safety Guardrails
- Technical Reliability

The framework connects:

**Creator Supply → Content Supply → Audience Engagement → Higher Time Spent → Stronger Creator Incentives**

## Approach

1. **Mapped the creator funnel** from discovering Reels creation to becoming a repeat creator.
2. **Defined the North Star Metric** around weekly active Reel creators to measure sustainable content supply.
3. **Identified leading indicators** such as discovery, CTA clicks, first-time creation, and first-publish rate.
4. **Tracked creator engagement and retention** through creation frequency, repeat creation, and creator retention.
5. **Added monetization and referral metrics** to measure long-term creator incentives and organic supply growth.
6. **Established guardrails** for content quality, spam, violations, deletion, and creator satisfaction.
7. **Included technical metrics** to ensure friction in the creation/upload experience does not constrain supply.

## Key Insights

- **Creator supply is the leading indicator of sustainable Reels growth**, while consumer time spent is an outcome.
- **Repeat creation matters more than first-time creation** for building a durable creator ecosystem.
- Creator retention and creation frequency reveal whether Instagram is successfully building **creator habit and stickiness**.
- **Quality guardrails are essential** — increasing content volume at the cost of quality can eventually hurt viewer engagement.
- Monetization and referrals can strengthen the **creator-side growth loop**, encouraging more creators to join and continue posting.

## Recommendations

- Make **Weekly Active Reel Creators** the primary creator-side health metric.
- Closely monitor **Repeat Creator Rate** as the key indicator of sustainable creator engagement.
- Optimize the funnel from **creation discovery → first publish → repeat creation**.
- Use **creator retention and creation frequency** to identify supply-side drop-offs early.
- Pair growth metrics with **quality, safety, satisfaction, and technical guardrails**.
- Invest in monetization and referral mechanisms that reinforce the creator flywheel.

## Business Impact

A creator-focused metrics framework helps Instagram identify **supply-side constraints before they impact audience engagement**.

**Healthier creator ecosystem → More consistent Reel creation → Greater content variety → Stronger viewer engagement → Higher time spent → Stronger creator incentives**

This creates a **self-reinforcing creator–audience flywheel** that can sustainably increase Instagram's overall time spent.

## Reflection

This project reinforced the importance of **measuring the constrained side of a two-sided platform**. Instead of focusing only on viewer consumption, I learned to identify creator supply as a critical leading indicator of long-term Reels growth.

With more time, I would extend the framework by defining **specific experiments and instrumentation plans** to identify which product interventions most effectively improve creator activation, repeat creation, and retention.
